Sales
April revenue of R102,916 was 20% below March, with Takealot (R43,016) and Payfast (R30,495) the main contributors. Reseller revenue dropped to R6,522 (March: R39,788), but a new Sales-Other line of R15,984 partially offset this. GP margin rose to 68.9% on a much leaner COGS month, with no manufacturing or significant packaging cost.
