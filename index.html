Revenue

Sales
June revenue of R77,356 fell 26% on May, with softness across most channels. Takealot was the exception and the largest channel at R53,029, up slightly on May, while Resellers dropped to R14,761 and Shopify eased to R5,667. Total boxes sold declined to 210 from 300.
